--- Recommendations ---

Key trade-offs:
  - Copper OFHC: Strongest repulsive force (highest conductivity), but heaviest.
    Best if mass is not the primary constraint.
  - Aluminum 6061: Good force, light, but lowest melting point.
    Risk of melting during prolonged safety engagement.
  - gamma-TiAl: Already on the cable for LIM plates. High service temp.
    Moderate force. A practical choice that avoids adding a new material.
  - Molybdenum: Highest melting point, good conductivity, extremely heavy.
    Best for absolute worst-case thermal survival.
  - Graphene/CNT composite: Lightest, highest temp tolerance in vacuum.
    Speculative conductivity. Best if projections hold.
