  Ring at 700 km: V_rel 8,825 m/s, casing 12,000 kg/m (95.0 kN/m), cable 119,592 kg/m, cable face 8.24 m.
  Two casing support tracks (1 m, persistent HTS), four compensation tracks of 1 m with 1.4 m plates: two lift (ceiling), two load (floor,

PART A: THE SLED PASSAGE WITH TWO 1 m TRACKS PER FACE AND STAGGERED 200 m COILS
==============================================================================
  5,000 t on 100 m at the front of a 10,000 m sled (525 kg/m), casing 12,000 kg/m, cable 119,592 kg/m, gap 100 mm.
  Spread pull: the cable's share (90.9 %) of the zone impulse over T = max(50 ms, zone/u), the bare sled cancelled in step;
  current regulated against the measured gap, 1 Hz loop on the casing support tracks, 2 ms rise time, no limiters.
  Coils: 20 per track, 200 m long, a new one every 10 m; a coil is on while the pull window overlaps it, at 1/20
  of the level, so the pull a point sees is the sum of the 20 coils over it: it ramps up over L_coil / u as they switch on one by one,
  holds, and ramps down, lasting T + 2 L_coil / u in all (the impulse is matched).  Track rating 573 kN/m per track (1.2 T mean),
  1,146 kN/m for a pair.  'both' = the pair shares the pull, 'one' = one track does it alone.
  Excursion = largest casing offset from the 100 mm gap during the pass and settling; plate field B = sqrt(2 mu0 F / width).

  B_pole = mean field normal to the plate over the pole faces, B_track = over the whole 1 m; B_plate = flux density along the plate through
  its thickness midway between poles (the flux that must flow sideways in the plate, the pole flux plus the slot leakage the plate shorts);
  W = stored energy per meter of track, gap and slots and leakage together; Bx = field across the track at the winding (along a flat-wound
  tape), By = field normal to the plate at the winding (through the tape face).  The slot field at the slot mouth is mu0 x slot ampere-turns
  / slot width; the maxima sit at the iron corners on the 5 mm grid.

  Air core.  The 20 coils flat and side by side with one polarity are one 1 m loop whose field at 100 mm is a few millitesla per 100 kA;
  alternating polarity at 50 mm pitch decays as exp(-pi x gap / pitch) and is gone at 100 mm; even an ideal 1 m sheet with its return far
  away gives 44 mT per 100 kA, so 0.74 T on the plate would need 1,691 kA-turns of one-way current per track,
  with the same field on the winding.  Without iron the coils cannot make the field.
